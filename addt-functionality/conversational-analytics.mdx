Conversational Analytics brings the power of natural language to your data warehouse. Instead of writing SQL queries or navigating dashboards, your team can simply ask questions in plain English and get instant answers.

## Semantic Layer

At the core of Conversational Analytics is a semantic layer that we maintain for you. This layer holds metric definitions and sits on top of your dimension and fact tables. The semantic layer knows about your attendance rates, assessment scores, enrollment counts, and all the other metrics that matter to your organization.

## AI Agents

Our AI agents connect directly to the semantic layer, allowing them to answer questions about your data and organization. When a user asks "What was our chronic absenteeism rate last month?", the agent understands the question, queries the appropriate metrics, and returns a clear answer.

## Gemini Enterprise Integration

Connect your semantic layer to platforms like Gemini Enterprise to bring Conversational Analytics directly into the tools your team already uses. Staff can ask questions about student data right from their Google Workspace environment without needing to switch to a separate analytics tool.
